# Movendo Latam design system

## Direction

Movendo needs to feel human, rigorous, and commercially clear. The visual system combines the editorial confidence of Novi with the structured conversion patterns of Afferolab: a warm neutral canvas, deep navy ink, a confident blue, and a single orange conversion accent.

## Visual tokens

- Background: warm off-white `#f7f4ee`
- Surface: clean ivory `#fffdf9`
- Ink: deep navy `#17212b`
- Primary: corporate blue `#164f9e`
- Accent: action orange `#f26b35`
- Highlight: soft chartreuse `#d9f26b`
- Muted text: slate `#5e6974`
- Border: cool neutral `#e5e7eb`

## Typography

- Display: Georgia / serif fallback with strong negative tracking for editorial headings.
- Body: Inter-like system sans stack for fast, readable B2B copy.
- Data: tabular sans numerals with generous spacing.

## Conversion principles

1. Make the promise visible before the scroll.
2. Put one primary CTA in the header and repeat it after proof.
3. Use proof points, case outcomes, and a concrete next step.
4. Keep the page calm enough to scan and specific enough to believe.
5. Use movement only to reveal structure, not to decorate.

## Accessibility checklist

- Mobile-first layout with no horizontal overflow.
- Semantic headings and labels.
- Visible focus states and 44px minimum interactive targets.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
- Meaningful images have alt text; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
- Form errors are adjacent to fields and announced with `aria-live`.
